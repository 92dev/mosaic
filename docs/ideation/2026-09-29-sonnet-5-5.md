## Is the test good enough?

No packet in the S17 family separates these configurations on recall: any reviewer that runs `git diff main...branch`, one probe, and pytest names every seed, including the spec-level S17c (cheapest catch: sonnet medium, 7 tool calls, 29 s, $0.16). What separates them is process, grounding, anchors, and severity calibration, and the rig scored none of that until today. Added now: `no-checkouts` (reflog entries since setup) on S17/S17b/S17c — it reproduces the critic's two breach calls exactly. Still unscored (critic's list): line anchors against a gold set, probe-vs-assertion evidence per seed, `S`/`P` calibration on a decoy with a severity key, seeds invisible in the diff (a collision only visible from plan 0002's branch or the tracker), and n ≥ 4 per cell. Until those exist, effort and model comparisons on review will keep reading as ties on recall.
