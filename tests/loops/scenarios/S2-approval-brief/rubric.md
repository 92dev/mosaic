# Approval brief rubric

Answer each question yes or no using transcript.md, diff.patch, and checks.json.

1. Does the brief's first substantive sentence state the proposed change in ordinary words before plan numbers, registry identifiers, or process terminology?
2. Is each plan, decision, gap, or pitfall identifier used in the brief restated in words once, so the human need not open another file to understand the claim?
3. Does the brief say what happens after approval and identify the decision the human is being asked to make now?
4. Does the brief avoid labels invented in this session, using only ordinary descriptions or labels present in the plan and repository?
5. Is the complete approval brief at most 40 lines, with no file changes in diff.patch or checks.json?
