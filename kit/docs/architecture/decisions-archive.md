# Decisions Archive (retired D#)

Map of retired decisions, moved out of the [decision map](README.md) by a signed-off audit or a superseding decision and frozen afterwards (`rule://records`, Archive class). Each retired decision keeps its number and its full section, moved verbatim to `archived/<element doc>.md` under an `**Archived:**` reason; numbers are never reused, and citations of a retired decision still resolve. Sessions do not preload this file: read [registries](../process/registries.md) and report hits as `archived:`, never as current truth.
